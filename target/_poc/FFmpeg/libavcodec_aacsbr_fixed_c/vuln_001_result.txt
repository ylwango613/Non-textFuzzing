either a higher SBR rate or a bypass of the max_qmf_subbands guard.

Verdict: The vulnerable code path IS reachable and the missing bounds check is
confirmed. At the standard parameter boundary (m[1]=48), the loop fills the arrays
to their exact capacity with no crash.
