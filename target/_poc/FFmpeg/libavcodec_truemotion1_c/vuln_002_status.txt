VERIFIED_BEHAVIOR

Bug confirmed active at truemotion1.c line 783:
  truemotion1_decode_24bit() reads mb_change_bits[mb_change_index++]
  unconditionally for every row, including keyframes.
  The 16-bit sibling (truemotion1_decode_16bit, line 656) guards
  this with `if (!keyframe)`.

Observable evidence from running vuln_002_input.avi through the ASAN-
enabled ffmpeg binary:

  [truemotion1] help! truemotion1 decoder went out of bounds

  (message appears twice — once during avformat_find_stream_info and
   once during decode).

The "help!" message originates from GET_NEXT_INDEX (line 532) and fires
prematurely because the missing keyframe guard at line 783 consumes index
stream bytes as mb_change_bits data, exhausting the 1-byte index stream
after the initial read rather than after proper decoding.

No ASAN heap-buffer-overflow was triggered.  Structural analysis shows
this is not reachable with the current codebase because GET_NEXT_INDEX
(which has its own bounds check) returns the function before mb_change_bits
can advance past the ASAN red-zone (AV_INPUT_BUFFER_PADDING_SIZE = 64 bytes).
The mb_change_bits advancement rate (1 byte per 4 rows for avctx_width=2)
is always exceeded by the index-stream consumption needed to survive those
rows, so the pointer never reaches the unprotected region.

Verdict: logic/state-corruption bug confirmed; direct ASAN heap-buffer-
overflow not achieved with minimal-packet approach.
