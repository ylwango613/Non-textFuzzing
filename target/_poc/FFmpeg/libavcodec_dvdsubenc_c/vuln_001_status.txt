The vulnerable check at dvdsubenc.c:344 is confirmed reachable with crafted PGS subtitle dimensions (w=1025, h=2046). The error "dvd_subtitle too big" / "Buffer too small" confirms the overflow guard is triggered. However, the integer overflow (w*h > INT_MAX) bypass cannot be achieved: av_image_check_size2() in ff_set_dimensions() (pgssubdec.c) and avcodec_open2() (avcodec.c) mathematically prevent any dimension pair where w*h >= 2^31 from reaching the dvdsub encoder. For AV_PIX_FMT_NONE, the constraint (8*w+1024)*(h+128) < INT_MAX limits max achievable w*h to ~264M, well below the 2.1B needed for signed int32 overflow. The vulnerability exists in code but cannot be triggered through standard PGS subtitle decoding in this FFmpeg build.
