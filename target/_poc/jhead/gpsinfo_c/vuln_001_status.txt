- a=0: reads [104..111] - within bounds (our crafted lat rational data)

         returns an uninitialized byte (0xBE = -66 signed) -> UBSAN fires
         "left shift of negative value -66"
- a=2: reads [120..135] - OOB further past ExifLength

ASAN does not fire because jpgfile.c:130 allocates malloc(itemlen+20) - jhead
adds 20 extra bytes to every section buffer to absorb short reads by fuzzers.
Our OOB extends 15 bytes past ExifLength, fully within the 20-byte cushion.
UBSAN, however, catches the UB caused by left-shifting the garbage negative
byte value recovered from that uninitialized memory.
